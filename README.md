# Free Transform display-profile evidence

Original synthetic brown, green and blue swatches rendered in PhotoCraft. No reporter artwork or private images are included.

Captured at d5e84c6 by cloud run38557574898. All six PNGs are byte-identical to the images visually inspected from run38554586630.

`before` means before opening Free Transform, `during` means while its handles are visible, and `after` means after committing a no-op transform. These are candidate captures, not before/after-fix comparisons. The baseline defect is established separately by the failing actual-texture regression in run38549665214.

Each profile has its own before/during/after comparison. Display P3 is simulated through the application's monitor-profile setting; this is not a physical monitor calibration test. Swatches are opaque; this does not demonstrate transparent blend parity.
